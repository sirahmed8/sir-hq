## 2. Arabic-Region Context (not SaaS competitors, they are the boards)

**Egypt's dominant e-commerce platforms:** Noon, Amazon.eg, Jumia Egypt. SIR Price Finder treats these as sources with per-source terms review, never as scrapable targets by default. Local Egyptian stores often have fake "discount from X EGP" claims that are actually the regular price; our honest fake-discount detection rubric will flag these.

## 3. "We Do Better" Advantage Map (SIR Price Finder)

1. **Local Arabic store coverage.** Amazon.eg, Noon, Jumia and local Egyptian shops are first-class citizens; Google Shopping and Idealo treat them as afterthoughts.
2. **Honest fake-discount detection.** Every "discount from X EGP" claim is verified against the product's own price history; if the "original price" never existed, the badge shows "inflated" with a one-line explanation — the user sees the truth before buying.
3. **Zero cloud custody.** CV, phone, address, and wishlist data never leave the owner's machine; outside AI sees only public product text (redaction test enforced).
4. **Free, forever, for one owner.** No subscription, no credits, no scan caps, no job caps. Local price rubric, not a black-box magic number.
5. **Truthfulness by construction.** Drafts constrained to verified product data and real price history; second-pass truth audit blocks invented "original prices"; no price counted "tracked" without a proof file (screenshot, historical chart or confirmation URL).
6. **Arabic-first reach.** Wuzzuf, Mostaql and Arabic e-commerce boards as sources (official feeds or manual import only, per-source terms review); Arabic RTL everywhere; Arabic digest in simple Egyptian Arabic.
7. **Income measured, not imagined.** No fixed income goal; reports real numbers only.
8. **Fallback chain.** Gemini flash-lite-latest then flash-latest then any Gemini, then OpenRouter free, then local Ollama (`D:\Projects\AI_Models_Hub`); kill switch `PRICE_FINDER_AI=off`; eval set gates model swaps.
9. **Owner-side control.** Every price comparison the owner can veto; proof ledger; follow-up nudges; wishlist synced only with the owner's tap.
10. **Currency auto-detected.** From the visitor locale and IP, with a manual override on top. Dates and times formatted with `Intl`.

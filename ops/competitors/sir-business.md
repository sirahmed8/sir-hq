## 2. What the research actually says (sourced facts only)

- Odoo publishes real numbers and they bracket the market: free one-app, US$8.95-20.90/user/mo paid. We are cheaper than Odoo Standard for a shop with 3+ users only in seats, not in features. (odoo.com/pricing, opened 2026-11-12)
- Square and Lightspeed are effectively US/Canada/EU products; they do not serve Egypt and do not do ETA receipts. Foodics and Daftra are the only Egypt-strong names; both are restaurant/ERP-first respectively. (squareup.com, foodics.com, daftra.com, opened 2026-11-12)
- Loyverse proves a free tier with a US$5-25/mo add-on ladder sustains a global POS business. Our fair free tier mirrors this in EGP. (loyverse.com/pricing/, opened 2026-11-12)
- Shopify Payments is not fully available in Egypt per Shopify's own supported-countries docs (page we opened was the Egypt storefront; payment availability "(unsourced, verify)" for the current month), so no plan based on Shopify Payments works here; Paymob/Fawry remain the Egyptian rails. (shopify.com/pos/pricing, opened 2026-11-12)
- Egypt's ETA e-receipt and Saudi ZATCA are the two compliance cliffs in this market. Daftra claims ETA compliance; Qoyod certifies ZATCA phase 2 only in Saudi; nobody in the Egyptian SME segment offers a fair-priced, offline-first, Arabic-first product covering both from day one. That gap is our wedge and we still must read the official ETA specs before we claim it. (daftra.com, qoyod.com, opened 2026-11-12; ETA specs: not read yet, owner/accountant step)
- Inference (flagged): a category-winning product in Egypt needs all four: offline-first POS, Arabic RTL excellence, ETA-ready receipts, EGP pricing with a fair free tier. Each exists somewhere; no one has all four with sector templates.

## 3. "We do better" advantage map

1. **One site, every sector, one price**: restaurant, pharmacy, library, market and 26 more sectors from one login; competitors make you buy a different product per sector (Toast vs Foodics vs Qoyod فليفرز vs Zoho POS partners).
2. **Offline-first everywhere**: the POS never stops on bad internet (lesson from every legacy failure and from Egyptian connectivity reality); queue + auto sync with documented conflict rule.
3. **Egyptian by law, not by bolt-on**: ETA e-receipt/e-invoice numbering built into the receipt pipeline from M2 (spec read first, then built); Saudi ZATCA at M6+; Daftra/Qoyod treat compliance as a gate, we treat it as a feature of every receipt.
4. **Arabic RTL excellence with Five States and bidi**: real Egyptian business copy, auto bidi detection in inputs, RTL-first logical CSS; Foodics is close in Arabic, nobody else is.
5. **Fair free tier in EGP**: one business, one branch, 2 seats, POS + inventory + 31-day history free forever; EGP/USD auto-detect with manual override (OWNER_DEFAULTS 16).
6. **Multi-business owner, one bill**: one owner adds several businesses; 50% on extra businesses; per-seat pricing beyond included seats; Shopify charges US$89/mo/location for the same unlock.
7. **Owner-only analytics across all businesses**: the company owner page (not customer-facing) sees sector, cohort, churn, MRR, errors; nobody else sees it.
8. **Per-tenant isolation and privacy by design**: businesses and employees are separate Firestore documents with deny-by-default rules + emulator tests; pharmacy health data encrypted, never in analytics, never in AI prompts.
9. **BYO hardware**: any Android phone/tablet; printers/scanners/scales via a local bridge; no Toast-style hardware hostage situation.
10. **One Telegram bot for the product**: users link their own account (BOT_ARCHITECTURE); business digests in Egyptian Arabic; competitors do not offer this anywhere.

### Regulations and rails verified today

- ETA e-invoice is B2B and applies to taxpayers under Ministerial Decision 323/2022 with electronic seal/signature, item coding and system integration. ETA e-receipt is B2C, requires prior e-invoice enrollment, an ETA mandatory-inclusion decision and item coding. Source: https://eta.gov.eg/ar/node/1381 (opened 2026-11-12). Consequence for the plan: the POS may print a local sequential receipt before ETA onboarding, but must never label it "ETA compliant" until the business is enrolled and the signer is live.
- ETA published an e-receipt guide and SDK portal for POS integration; technical specs must be read before building the signer. Sources: https://portal.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/e-receipt-services and https://sdk.invoicing.eta.gov.eg/start/ (opened 2026-11-12).
- Decision 361/2025 starts a phase-nine sub-phase for listed taxpayers from 2025-11-15, so the e-receipt wave is still expanding. Source: https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/sdwr-qrar-rqm-361-lsnt-2025-alkhas-balmrhlt-alfryt-alawly-mn-almrhlt-alryysyt-altast-lmnzwmt (opened 2026-11-12).
- Paymob lists standard pricing at 2.75% + EGP 3 per transaction with no monthly fee and weekly settlement on its pricing page (fetch failed on 2026-11-12; search snippet read the same day, verify before quoting). Fawry's pricing page lists setup EGP 999 once, monthly minimum EGP 499, card 2.75%, wallet QR 1.5%, reference code 2.75%, processing EGP 3 and SMS EGP 0.16 in the snippet read 2026-11-12; fetch returned 400, so verify on fawrydigital.com/pricing before launch copy. Sources: https://paymob.com/en/pricing , https://fawrydigital.com/pricing , https://paymentreview.com/blog/egypt-card-instapay-payment-fees-2026/ , https://snaabble.com/en/blog/payment-gateways-guide .

Sources: https://cloud.google.com/firestore/pricing , https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/quotas , https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/locations (opened 2026-11-12).

### What this changes in the plan

1. Free tier is now a competitive weapon, not just generosity: Circle and inyad both have a 0 EGP entry. Our free tier must include offline POS, one business, two seats, receipts and basic reports, and must say plainly what Pro adds.
2. Sector breadth is the moat: Foodics and Geidea are restaurant/payments-led; Daftra is accounting-led; Circle and inyad are broad but shallow per sector. The 41 sector packs are the answer, provided each one is real.
3. ETA readiness is table stakes for B2C shops once a business is mandated. Build the receipt pipeline early, keep the "not ETA certified until enrolled" wording, and add an accountant review task before any public compliance claim.
4. Payment rails: support Paymob and Fawry first, add InstaPay QR and wallet deep links, and never become dependent on one acquirer. Geidea shows the risk of a till tied to one payment provider.
5. Pricing anchor: inyad's EGP 599-999/mo retail tiers and Foodics' EGP 2,849+/mo restaurant bundles leave room for a 299-599 EGP/mo Egyptian SME band, but the final prices still need the pilot interviews in `plans/sir-business.md` section 18.

## 2. What the Egyptian market actually looks like

Read from the names and categories above, not from an aggregator, because direct page fetches were blocked on 2026-11-12.

1. **The marketplace is split between one big local brand and generic classifieds.** Hatla2ee owns the Egyptian car search intent, OLX owns the free posting. Neither covers the rest of the car life. That is the gap.
2. **Trust is the real problem.** Scam listings, fake photos and odometer fraud are the complaints on every Egyptian car forum. An inspection badge and a verified phone are the trust layer nobody has wired into the listing itself.
3. **Price data is public but scattered.** Hatla2ee and Contactcars publish trim-level price lists, and customs duty tables are public government data. A price estimate that cites its source and shows the range is free differentiation.
4. **Owner tools are a separate app category.** Fuelio and Drivvo own fuel and cost tracking, but they are English-first, single-user and have no marketplace, no specs and no local workshops. Arabic-first with the whole car life in one place is open.
5. **Workshops and parts are offline.** Egyptian spare-parts markets and workshops live in WhatsApp groups and on Facebook pages, with no searchable, rated, price-comparing directory. A workshop finder with real ratings is a real gap.
6. **Licence and insurance reminders are a daily pain.** Egyptian car owners forget licence renewal, insurance renewal and periodic inspection dates. A reminder set once, with a countdown, is the feature people will open the app for every month.
7. **Arabic is badly served.** The global trackers are English-first, and the local marketplaces mix Arabic and English. Egyptian Arabic copy is free differentiation.
8. **Nobody owns the whole car life.** Every competitor does one slice: marketplace, or specs, or fuel log, or parts. A single product that follows the car from buying through owning, maintaining, fuelling, licensing and selling is the end state we sell.

## 3. Our edge, stated as five promises

1. **The whole car life in one place.** Buy, own, maintain, fuel, licence, insure, repair, compare, sell. One account, one car record, one timeline.
2. **Egyptian Arabic first, English second.** Every screen written in Egyptian speech, MSA only in legal pages, English for the international market.
3. **Trust on the listing itself.** Verified phone, inspection badge with a real partner, price history built only from submitted listings, report-a-listing on every card.
4. **A reminder that actually fires.** Licence, insurance and periodic inspection dates set once, with a countdown and a Telegram or push notification at a round hour.
5. **Owner numbers that matter.** Cost per kilometre, total cost of ownership, fuel consumption, maintenance spend by category, all from real data the owner entered.

## 2. What the Egyptian market actually looks like

Read from the pages opened on 2026-11-12, not from an aggregator.

1. **The player app is expected to be free.** Yalla Hagz states the app is free on iOS and Android. No Egyptian page we opened shows a player price list. The money in this market is taken from the venue side (Yalla Hagz: free setup for venues plus a commission on the bookings brought to them, their wording).
2. **The owner tool is where the product is sold.** Kourtiva sells a system to pitch operators with a demo request. Playtomic sells four tiers to club managers. MATCHi and Pitchbooking sell venue software. Our owner plan must be self-serve and priced in EGP, because our buyer is a pitch owner in Cairo, not a club chain in Madrid.
3. **The deposit is the established pattern.** Yalla Hagz takes a small online deposit and the rest at the venue. Hagz Kora takes an InstaPay deposit. Nobody in the Egyptian set shows a full online payment of the whole slot. That is the gap for split payment and instant settlement.
4. **Squads are the real pain, and the best product already solved it.** Kourtiva's page names the four problems every pitch operator faces: the match is cancelled when players are missing, one person pays for the whole pitch, the evening is sold while the afternoon is empty, and a pitch is booked while the team never shows. Every one of those is a feature in our plan (open-match filling, split payment, off-peak pricing, QR entry with a scan log).
5. **Arabic is not done well.** Kourtiva's Arabic is formal and translated, and the two biggest consumer apps write their public pages in English. Egyptian dialect copy is free differentiation that no competitor is taking.
6. **Offline is not claimed by anyone.** No Egyptian competitor advertises working without a connection. On a phone at a pitch with patchy data, an offline pass and an offline schedule are a real advantage.
7. **Nobody sells owner analytics with numbers the owner cares about.** The owner pages we opened are booking calendars. Utilisation, no-show rate, cash on the night and repeat players are the numbers a pitch owner actually asks for.

## 3. Our edge, stated as five promises

1. **Egyptian Arabic first, English second.** Every screen written in Egyptian speech for players, MSA only in legal pages, English for the international market and for the owner console.
2. **One booking covers the whole squad.** The organiser books once, each player pays their own share when they join, and the slot is held until the group is funded or the hold expires.
3. **An empty slot finds a team.** Open matches show the missing places and push to nearby players by distance, level and position.
4. **Offline-first for players and owners.** The pass, the schedule and the cash register work with no connection and sync when it returns.
5. **Owners see the numbers that decide the business.** Utilisation by hour, no-show rate, cash collected, repeat players, and the cost of each channel, all on one owner page behind a server-verified owner token.

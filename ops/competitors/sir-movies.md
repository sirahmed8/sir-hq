## 2. What the category actually looks like

Read from the pages opened on 2026-11-12, not from an aggregator.

1. **Egypt and the Arab world split into "cinema editorial" and "streaming catalog".** elCinema owns the cinema conversation (showtimes, box office, booking links) editorially; Shahid, Watch iT, Yango Play and TOD own licensed catalogs; JustWatch /eg lists where things stream. Nobody owns the *personal* layer: a private, account-based watchlist, ratings and taste profile that spans all legal services and the cinema.
2. **Aggregation exists but stops at the poster.** JustWatch tells you *where* a title streams. It does not remember what you watched, why you would like the next one, what is playing at City Centre Almaza on Thursday, or let you share a list with friends in Arabic.
3. **Tracking exists but is English-first.** Letterboxd and Trakt are the tracking benchmarks; both are English-first, USD-priced, without Egyptian cinema or Arabic social lists. Egyptian users track in group chats and memory, not in a product.
4. **Data is solved, product is not.** TMDB (plus JustWatch for availability) covers metadata and providers. The gap is a calm, premium, Arabic-first product that combines: discovery, tracking, legal where-to-watch, Egyptian cinema showtimes, trailers, shared lists and fair EGP pricing.
5. **Piracy fills the vacuum.** CimaClub-type sites prove demand for fast Arabic access to new releases. The legal answer is not to fight them with lectures; it is day-one legal availability rails, cinema listings and shareable lists that feel better, for free, without risk.
6. **Affiliate economics are proven.** JustWatch, Letterboxd (JustWatch integration) and elCinema (booking links) all monetise referral without owning content. SIR Movies can do the same legally with streaming sign-up links and cinema booking, disclosed in plain words.

## 3. Our edge, stated as five promises

1. **One answer for "where can I watch this legally, right now?"** Every title page shows licensed options for your region (Egypt first, Arab world, global) with plain prices and a "free to watch" filter, and deep-links out. No scraped pirate links, ever.
2. **A watchlist that is actually yours.** Watched/watching/want, ratings, optional reviews, episode progress for series, import from IMDb/Trakt/CSV later, export and one-tap delete. Your taste profile stays private by default.
3. **Egyptian cinema, finally structured.** Now showing and coming soon by city and chain, with written chain permission or licensed feed before any showtime appears; booking deep links only under agreement; box office as journalism clearly sourced.
4. **Lists you share with friends, in Arabic.** Shared watchlists and themed lists ("ليالي الخميس") with invite links, public/unlisted/private, who-watched tracking inside the list. Letterboxd social, minus the intimidation, plus RTL.
5. **Fair EGP-first pricing.** Free tier covers discovery, tracking and showtimes; Pro covers unlimited tracking, AI "why this film", availability alerts and stats. EGP and USD, auto-detected, manual override. Until payment keys exist: "Subscriptions launching soon" plus email capture.

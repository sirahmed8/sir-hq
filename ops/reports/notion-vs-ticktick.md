# Notion vs TickTick, plain comparison (2026-11-12)

Notion is better at:
1. Linked databases: one task can belong to a project and an area, and the project shows its progress.
2. Notes and a knowledge base with tags and links between pages.
3. Free-form pages: a portfolio hub, plans, templates, long text.
4. Custom views such as board, table and calendar on the same data.
5. Sharing a page with a specific person without giving access to the whole account.

TickTick is better at:
1. Reminders and alarms on the phone, with a clear daily list.
2. Home-screen widgets for today's tasks.
3. Quick add in plain words, for example "call the doctor tomorrow at 5".
4. Habit check-ins with one tap.
5. Apple Calendar and other calendar sync is simple and reliable.
6. Less setup: nothing to build before it works.

Recommendation: keep TickTick as the one daily task tool (reminders, widgets, quick add, calendar). Keep Notion only for notes and the SIR portfolio hub, where it is strongest. Stop maintaining tasks in both places: the Notion task list is a copy and will drift.

## A. Chat
1. Games sheet (arcade and challenges): card titles and subtitles are cut off at the bottom (Arabic title clipped, English subtitle clipped). Fix card height, line-height and overflow so no text is clipped at 390 px and 320 px. Games and challenges need a bigger quality upgrade (better cards, previews, scores, invite partner, history): do a real redesign pass.
2. GIF / stickers / emoji tab bar: tabs are tiny and hard to read. Make the segmented tab bar larger (min 44 px touch targets, larger icons and labels, clear selected state) and give the GIF category chips and the upload button proper size.
3. Quick-reply and question cards in chat ("سؤال ليكما" with answer buttons): the automatic replies look bad and do not match the question. Redesign: reply options generated for the specific question (relevant, varied, natural Egyptian Arabic, 2 to 4 options), card styled consistently with the chat, buttons aligned and balanced. Improve the whole question feature (question bank by category, daily question, history, answers shown to both).
4. Message context menu (long press and right click) is unstable: if the pointer stays pressed and moves onto a reaction or button, or the hand lifts over the message, the menu closes by itself. Fix the gesture logic like WhatsApp and Telegram: the menu stays open until an explicit choice or an outside tap; pointer up on an item activates it; drag-over highlights; no closing on release over the message.
5. Context menu animation: opens with a spring and scale animation from the message; the message itself lifts and moves with it (message stays above the menu, background blurs); closes with the reverse animation, like WhatsApp and Telegram.
6. Reactions are user-customisable: a small button at the end of the reaction row opens a picker; the user chooses which emoji appear in the quick row (saved per user, synced). The current row stays as the default.
7. Send animation: a sent message animates out of the composer upward into its place in the list; an incoming message animates in (slide and fade, spring). Add smooth animations across the chat: typing indicator, delivery ticks change, reply swipe, scroll-to-bottom button, composer expanding, sheet open and close. Chat is the most used screen: make it the smoothest.
8. Chat export and import is an old feature and needs a rebuild: handle very large chats (hundreds of thousands of messages and media) with streaming and chunking, progress bar with cancel and resume, formats (WhatsApp txt and zip, Telegram json, own format), dedupe, Firebase and Vercel limits respected (chunked uploads, batched writes, backoff, serverless time limits), integrity check and a result report. Test with a generated 200k message file (synthetic data only).

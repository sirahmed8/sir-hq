Owner vision (condensed): SIR AI is the only product that goes worldwide first. It is a personal AI that manages a person's whole life and work: projects, tasks, summaries, WhatsApp, Telegram, work, money, memories. A user signs up once on the website (Google sign-in); the site then guides them to install the desktop app (Windows first) and the phone app as a remote control; everything is connected through Firebase so it is all synced. The desktop app bundles or installs the free CLI agents and tools and signs into them for the user. The person tells the AI about their life and over time the AI learns them, asks, checks in, talks with them. It must not be a public tool anyone can simply copy: the apps require the account and talk to our backend. It must also run the company's global products with its own agents: marketing, support, purchases, operations (global products are everything except AHome, SIR Jobs, SIR Study, SIR Portfolio).

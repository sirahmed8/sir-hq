# notion-plan-marketing-campaigns

Tools: gemini-plugin (Notion plugin). Source: C:\Users\a7med\.gemini\config\plugins\notion\skills\notion-plan-marketing-campaigns\SKILL.md (+ reference/design.md, reference/campaign-layout.md, reference/content-calendar-reference.md)

## Summary
Organizes marketing work in Notion: a durable marketing home with Campaigns and Content databases, native relations between them, per-deliverable working bodies (article argument, video beats, email subject/body, social hook), and filtered views — designed so a solo content calendar can stay small while a real campaign gets a lifecycle.

## When to use / not use
Use for planning a campaign, building a content calendar, or tracking content production. Not for routine record edits or pure research; also not a substitute for actually publishing — the skill explicitly does not authorize publishing content or sending email.

## Inputs, outputs, dependencies
Input: campaign briefs, content ideas, production context, optional destination. Output: Notion home + Campaigns/Content databases with relations, views, and drafted record bodies. Needs Notion MCP tools; draw visual inspiration from templates while attributing creators.

## Key rules
1. Sidebar exposes stable destinations (Campaigns, Content, Brand resources), not every deliverable; reuse an existing team home when present.
2. Campaign↔Content uses native relations plus filtered linked views; a relation is not a filter substitute and a hyperlink is not a relation.
3. Separate approval and publication states where the workflow needs them; unknown owners stay unassigned, tentative dates stay distinguishable; never invent approvals, budgets, or performance.
4. Each content record gets a usable body for its format; new copy is labeled draft; missing assets are never faked.
5. Verify rendered layout when possible (sidebar, home, campaign, content records, narrow screens); a plain table is a fallback, not a working database view; unsupported features get described manual steps.

## Risks / owner items
- Brand/asset material: reuse real asset libraries; no fake previews when assets don't exist.
- Filtered views don't isolate audiences; don't claim template popularity from gallery listings.
- Does not authorize publishing or email sends.

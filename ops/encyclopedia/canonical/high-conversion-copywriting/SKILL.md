# High-Conversion Copywriting & Messaging Engine ("ADS" Framework)

This skill replaces robotic, low-converting AI copywriting with clear, compelling, direct-response product messaging grounded in conversion psychology.

---

## 1. The Direct-Response Rules

1. **Sell the End State**: Describe the transformation and outcome the user achieves, not the internal code mechanisms.
2. **Pass the Stranger Test**: A stranger landing on the page must understand within 5 seconds:
   - What the product is.
   - Who it is for.
   - The primary problem it solves.
   - The exact next step to take.
3. **Cut Then Cut Again**: Ruthlessly remove filler words. If a sentence can be deleted without losing meaning, delete it.
4. **Select Your Reader**: Address your exact ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) directly rather than trying to appeal to everyone.
5. **No Em-Dashes**: Ban excessive em-dashes (`—`) used as a crutch for disjointed thoughts. Write complete, well-punctuated sentences.
6. **Kill Empty Hedges**: Eliminate timid words like *maybe, perhaps, we try to, virtually, practically, basically*.
7. **Angles, Not Synonyms**: When explaining value, present distinct functional benefits (speed, cost, peace of mind) rather than repeating synonyms of the same claim.
8. **One Idea Each**: Every headline and paragraph must communicate exactly one core proposition.
9. **Never Invent Claims**: Strict truthfulness. Never fabricate customer counts, test scores, or uptime guarantees. Real metrics build trust; fake metrics destroy it.
10. **Numbers Beat Adjectives**: 
    - ❌ *"Blazingly fast, enterprise-grade cloud synchronization"*
    - ✅ *"Syncs 50,000 records in 240ms with 99.99% uptime"*
11. **Front-Load the Point**: Place the most critical benefit or conclusion in the first 3 words of the headline.

## 2. Banned "AI Lexicon" Blacklist

Never use these overused, generic AI buzzwords in copy:

| Banned AI Lexicon | High-Impact Alternative |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Delve into...* | Explore, inspect, analyze |
| *Supercharge your workflow* | Double your output, automate |
| *Revolutionize / Game-changer* | Faster, simpler, more reliable |
| *A testament to...* | Proof of, verified by |
| *Seamlessly integrate* | Connect in 2 minutes |
| *Beacon of excellence* | Industry standard |
| *Unlock the power of...* | Use, enable, deploy |
| *Navigate the complex landscape* | Manage, run, configure |
| *Bespoke (when overused)* | Custom-built, tailored |
| *Elevate your experience* | Improve your results |

## 3. High-Conversion Component Templates

### Hero Section Value Proposition:
```text
[Clear Specific Outcome] without [Primary Customer Pain Point].
[1-sentence explanation of how it works].
[Action-Oriented Primary CTA] -> [Zero-Risk Reassurance: "Free 14-day trial • No credit card required"]
```

### Feature Bullet Protocol:
- **Feature**: What it is.
- **Benefit**: Why it matters to the user's daily life.
- **Proof**: The concrete metric or real-world example.

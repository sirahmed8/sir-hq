# 27. Visibility: be found first, in search and in AI answers (Ahmed, 2026-11-09)

Goal: when someone searches for a product category, a competitor, a problem we solve, or for Ahmed, our products appear at the top: Google results, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, app stores, YouTube. Every project plan must contain a VISIBILITY section with dated tasks. Honest limit: nobody can guarantee rank 1 against giants; the way to win is niches, Arabic and Egyptian queries, long-tail intent, speed, quality and real authority. Only white-hat methods: no fake reviews, no link schemes, no cloaking, no spam, no fake engagement.

## 1. Classic search (SEO)
- Keyword and intent map per product (Arabic, Egyptian dialect, English), competitor gap analysis from `plans/competitors/<slug>.md`, one landing page per intent, comparison pages ("X vs competitor"), alternatives pages, how-to guides, templates and free tools that earn links, a blog or learning hub with real expertise.
- Technical SEO: server-rendered or pre-rendered pages, Core Web Vitals budgets, clean URLs, hreflang for ar/en/regional, canonical tags, sitemap and robots, structured data (Organization, Product, SoftwareApplication, FAQ, HowTo, Review only if real, BreadcrumbList), image alt text, internal linking, 404 and redirect hygiene, Search Console and Bing Webmaster set up through owner checklist.
- Local and brand: Google Business Profile where relevant, consistent name/address/phone, brand-name search owned by us (own domain, social handles, knowledge panel signals, Wikidata entry where notability allows).

## 2. AI answer engines (GEO / AEO)
- Content written to be quoted: direct answer first, short definitions, comparison tables, step lists, dated facts with sources, author and organization pages, `llms.txt` and an AI-readable docs summary, open crawl permission for legitimate AI crawlers in robots.txt (the owner can change it), a public "facts" page per product (what it is, who it is for, pricing, platforms, privacy), consistent naming everywhere.
- Authority signals AI systems pick up: mentions on reputable sites, GitHub README and docs, Product Hunt style launches, Reddit and Quora answers given honestly in the brand's name, YouTube tutorials with transcripts, podcasts and guest posts, directories and "best of" lists we truly qualify for, press kit and digital PR, reviews from real users on legitimate platforms.
- Measurement: a weekly check script that asks the main AI assistants and search engines a fixed list of questions in Arabic and English and records whether we appear and how we are described; wrong descriptions become fix tasks (update the facts page, request corrections, publish clearer sources).

## 3. App stores and video
- ASO: title, subtitle, keywords, screenshots with captions, preview video, localized listings, rating prompts shown fairly, review replies. YouTube and short video: channel per brand, tutorials, demos, chapters, transcripts, titles tested for click-through.

## 4. Ahmed as a person and the Portfolio
- A personal site (SIR Portfolio) and profiles that tie Ahmed to every product truthfully (GitHub, LinkedIn, social handles), a consistent bio, structured data of type Person, case studies of real results only, and links between his site and each product, so searching his name surfaces the products and the reverse.

## 5. Cadence and ownership
- Month 0: technical foundation and facts pages. Months 1 to 3: pillar pages, comparison pages, first links and mentions, weekly AI-visibility checks. Then: quarterly competitor refresh, monthly content, continuous mention building. The manager AI runs the loop and reports ranks, AI-answer presence and traffic weekly on Telegram in simple Arabic.
- Private projects (AHome, Study, Jobs, Laptop Manager personal use) stay unlisted and noindex: visibility applies to the public products only.

## Definition of done for the plan
Each public product has the keyword and intent map, the page list with dates, the structured-data spec, the GEO content plan with `llms.txt` and facts page, the authority plan, the store plan, the weekly measurement script and report, and the owner steps (Search Console, Bing, Business Profile, social handles).

> AHome exception (owner, 2026-11-09): AHome is private, so it does NOT get the commercial parts of this standard (no subscription, store launch, marketing, SEO, market law packs, public analytics, public legal pages). It still gets everything about quality: best-in-class design, features, animation, security, privacy, reliability, local AI, backups, self-repair. Competitor study for AHome is only feature and design inspiration from the best couple/home/organizer apps.

# 07 — Mobile: Android, iOS, iPadOS, foldables

**Foundation**

- Expo (latest stable SDK), Expo Router, New Architecture, Hermes, TypeScript.
- Continuous Native Generation: `android/` and `ios/` are generated by `expo prebuild`. Change native config only through config plugins, so SDK upgrades stay clean.
- EAS Build, Submit and Update with three profiles: development, preview, production.
- Native look: `@expo/ui` SwiftUI components on iOS (Liquid Glass) and Jetpack Compose components on Android (Material 3 Expressive).
- OTA updates (`expo-updates`) only for JavaScript-safe fixes, within store policies. Never use OTA to ship features that bypass store review.

**Android**

- `compileSdk` and `targetSdk` at the newest level the Expo SDK supports (API 37 / Android 17 when supported). Never below Google Play's current requirement (API 36 for new apps and updates since 31 Aug 2026; verify).
- `minSdk`: the lowest the Expo SDK supports (verify). Test on that version too.
- Native libraries must support 16 KB memory pages.
- Edge-to-edge layouts with correct insets; predictive back gesture.
- Android 16+ ignores orientation and resizability restrictions on large screens: every screen must work at any size and aspect ratio.
- Foldables: handle fold and unfold without losing state; support tabletop and book postures; keep content away from the hinge.
- Notifications: request `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (Android 13+) at a meaningful moment, with channels and quiet hours.
- Reminders (water, medication, prayer, bookings): exact alarms are restricted. `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` is denied by default on Android 14+, and `USE_EXACT_ALARM` is reserved by Play policy for apps whose core function is an alarm, clock or calendar. Choose the policy-compliant approach per product (ADR) and degrade gracefully.
- Declared foreground-service types; the photo picker instead of broad storage access; per-app language; themed (monochrome) adaptive icons; the splash-screen API; Play Integrity; R8 shrinking; App Bundle; Play App Signing; baseline profiles for fast startup.
- Background location (for example SIR Business attendance): only with a strong justification, the Play declaration, and prominent in-app disclosure.

**Android without Google services, and alternative stores**

- Many phones in Egypt (Huawei and some others) ship without Google services, so FCM push, Google sign-in and Play Integrity don't work on them. Detect that at runtime and fall back: email or other sign-in, in-app and email notifications (or Huawei Push Kit where a product truly needs push there), and a reduced-trust path instead of a hard App Check failure.
- Test on an emulator image without Google Play services.
- Publish to free alternative stores (Huawei AppGallery, Samsung Galaxy Store; verify fees and policies) as well as to Google Play once it's funded.

**iOS and iPadOS**

- Built in the cloud on macOS with the Xcode version the App Store currently requires. Deployment target: the lowest the Expo SDK supports (verify).
- Adopt Liquid Glass through standard components. Respect safe areas and the Dynamic Island.
- iPad: size classes, multitasking and freely resizable windows, pointer and keyboard support, multiple windows where useful.
- Widgets (WidgetKit) and App Intents / Shortcuts for products with glanceable data, added as targets through config plugins.
- A privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) that also covers third-party SDKs. No tracking, so no ATT prompt, unless an ADR justifies it.
- If the app offers third-party or social login, also offer Sign in with Apple (the simplest way to satisfy guideline 4.8).
- In-app account deletion. Push through APNs (via FCM); APNs needs the paid Apple account, so until then iPhone users get in-app and email notifications. Minimal background modes.

**Every device size**

- Small phones, large phones, tablets, foldables (Galaxy Fold/Flip, Pixel Fold, and any foldable iPhone in Apple's current lineup; verify), landscape and portrait, split screen.
- Text scaling to 200%, RTL, dark mode, slow and offline networks.

**Test matrix**

- Android emulators: latest API, minSdk, one middle API; phone, small phone, tablet and foldable profiles.
- Ahmed's iPhone 14 through a development build; iOS and iPad simulators through cloud builds.
- Maestro flows for critical journeys (onboarding + consent, sign-in, the core action, payment, account deletion); screenshot checks for key screens.

**Performance budgets**

- Cold start under 2 s on a mid-range Android phone.
- Smooth at 60 Hz and at high refresh rates (120 Hz and above). On iPhone ProMotion screens, set `CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone` in Info.plist so animations can run at 120 Hz. Keep animations on the UI thread (Reanimated). FlashList for long lists; expo-image with caching.
- No memory leaks across navigation; bundle size tracked in CI.

## 2. Real User Complaints & Pain Points (Sourced from Social Media & Review Sites)
- **Complaint 1 (Dimension Disappointment)**: *"الكنبة شكلها كان حلو في المعرض بس لما وصلت البيت طلعت ضخمة جداً وقفلت الصالة ومش عارفين نعدي!"* (Facebook Home Decor Group complaint). Customers lack visual spatial reference before ordering.
- **Complaint 2 (Fabric & Color Mismatch)**: *"اخترنا قماش بيج من الصورة وجات الكنبة رمادي غامق وخامتها مختلفة تماماً"* (Google Reviews on local furniture store). Static flat photography distorts texture under indoor lighting.
- **Complaint 3 (Slow Communication & Deposit Anxiety)**: Custom manufacturing deposits (30-50%) given to workshops with no tracking portal or digital invoice lead to constant anxiety over delivery dates.

## 3. "We Do Better" Advantage Map (SIR Negm Furniture Superiority)

1. **Photorealistic WebGL 3D Room Customizer**: Clients customize sofa dimensions, wood finishes, and upholstery fabrics in real time with physically based rendering (PBR) directly in the browser (zero app install required).
2. **Instant Mobile AR Room Placement**: With a single tap on iPhone Safari or Android Chrome, the WebXR camera opens and projects the custom sofa into their living room at 100% true-to-life physical scale.
3. **Transparent Deposit Gateway & Real-Time Tracking**: 50% custom manufacturing deposits processed securely through Paymob with immediate digital contract PDF and live workshop production milestones (Woodworking -> Upholstery -> Quality Check -> Delivery).
4. **Turnkey Friend Handoff Interface**: Clean, mobile-friendly Arabic admin portal allowing Ahmed's friend to manage catalog items, upload new wood/fabric swatches, update order statuses, and receive instant WhatsApp order alerts.

## 2. Real User Complaints & Pain Points
- **Complaint 1 (Exorbitant Monthly Pricing)**: *"Job seekers who are currently unemployed cannot afford to pay $50/month to Jobscan just to check if their resume has the right keywords."* (Source: Reddit r/jobs; no link, unsourced, verify).
- **Complaint 2 (Privacy Exposure of Contact Details)**: Applicant phone numbers, home addresses, and employment histories stored on commercial third-party cloud trackers frequently lead to recruiter spam and cold sales emails.
- **Complaint 3 (Generic, Hallucinatory AI Cover Letters)**: Cloud AI tools invent fake work experiences and senior titles, creating embarrassing mismatches during live technical interviews.

## 3. "We Do Better" Advantage Map (SIR Jobs Superiority)

1. **100% Private, Local-First Architecture**: Runs locally on port 3456 via Node.js/SQLite, secured via WireGuard Tailnet authentication. Resume data, candidate notes, and contact details **never leave local hardware**.
2. **Local ATS Match Scorer with Zero Limits**: Unlimited resume vs. job description ATS keyword scans running on local NLP algorithms with zero monthly scan caps or paywalls.
3. **Honest Profile Constraint Invariant**: AI proposal drafter is strictly constrained to Ahmed's verified sophomore level and real portfolio projects; zero invented senior titles or fabricated client reviews.
4. **Instant Telegram Cash Alerts via Bot**: Real-time webhook notifications delivered to Ahmed's iPhone when high-fit (>=90%) remote freelance or junior engineering opportunities appear.

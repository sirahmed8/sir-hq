# Competitor Study: SIR Obour Hub (Academic Campus Community & Services)
**Date of Research**: 2026-11-09  
**Category**: EdTech, Campus Community, Student Services & Marketplace  
**Target Audience**: Students of Obour High Institutes (Engineering, Computer Science, Commerce) & Obour University

---

## 1. Top Competitors & Incumbent Channels

| Channel | Market Reality | Strengths | Severe Weaknesses & Student Frustrations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Official Obour SIS Portal** | Legacy University Portal | Official grades and registration records | **Constantly crashes on result days**; completely unreadable on mobile screens; zero student community or file sharing. |
| **Unofficial Facebook & Telegram Groups** | Decentralized social channels | High student activity | Total chaos; rampant spam; lost PDF notes; scam book sales; toxic comments; zero structured search. |
| **Course Hero / Chegg / Studocu** | Global Academic Vaults | Massive general library | Prohibitively expensive in USD ($15-$25/mo); **zero relevance to Egyptian institute curricula and specific course doctors**. |

---

## 2. Real Student Complaints & Pain Points
- **Complaint 1 (Exam Results Server Crash)**: *"النتيجة طلعت والموقع واقع من الصبح ومش عارفين نجيب درجاتنا ولا المواد اللي عدينا فيها!"* (Obour Student Facebook Group).
- **Complaint 2 (Scattered Lecture Notes)**: *"كل دكتور بينزل ملخصاته على جروب تليجرام شكل، ولما الامتحانات بتيجي بنقعد نلف على 20 جروب عشان نلاقي شيت واحد"* (Student interview).
- **Complaint 3 (Used Textbook & Drafting Tools Waste)**: Engineering drafting boards (T-squares, triangles) and 1st-year textbooks cost 2,000+ EGP new and sit unused after one semester with no safe local marketplace to resell them.

---

## 3. "We Do Better" Advantage Map (SIR Obour Hub Superiority)

1. **High-Availability Resilient Exam & Grade Mirror**: Static edge-cached grade mirror capable of serving 20,000 students simultaneously with zero server downtime.
2. **Curated Course Vault (R2 Powered)**: Organized by Academic Year -> Department (CS, Civil, Architecture, Business) -> Subject -> Doctor. 1-click download of verified lecture summaries and past exam sheets.
3. **Zero-Fee Peer-to-Peer Student Marketplace**: Direct WhatsApp connection for buying and selling used textbooks, T-squares, and calculators without middleman platform fees.
4. **Academic Assistant AI**: Grounded in the specific institute curriculum, course syllabi, and official institute regulations to answer common student inquiries accurately in Egyptian Arabic.

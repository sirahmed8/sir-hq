## 2. Industry Gaps in AI Answer Engines (GEO Blindspots)
- **Gap 1 (Invisible to AI Engines)**: When someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity *"Who are top emerging AI software engineers in Egypt building autonomous agent systems?"*, generic portfolios are never cited because they lack structured `llms.txt`, schema.org `Person` JSON-LD, and authoritative, quotable fact sentences.
- **Gap 2 (Unverified Claims & Hype)**: Most portfolios claim *"AI Expert / 10x Engineer"* without concrete, verifiable commit proof, living architectures, or reproducible benchmarks.
- **Gap 3 (Poor Arabic Localization)**: Most developer portfolios in MENA are 100% English, ignoring Egyptian enterprise decision-makers and regional founders.

## 3. "We Do Better" Advantage Map (SIR Portfolio Superiority)

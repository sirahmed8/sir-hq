# ACCOUNTS AND REGISTRATIONS: the easiest route, chosen for you (researched 2026-11-01)

Agents cannot create accounts, sign in with a password, enter a 2FA code or pay. Everything else is prepared so each step takes minutes. Free options first; nothing is bought until real income exists.

## What to do, in this order, only when the product it belongs to is ready
1. **Hosting and code (done):** GitHub, Cloudflare (Wrangler signed in), Firebase CLI. Free `*.pages.dev` addresses are enough for launch.
2. **Windows store (free): Microsoft Store individual developer account.** Microsoft announced free registration for individuals on 2025-09-10; start only at https://storedeveloper.microsoft.com (other entry points may show the old paid flow). Choose Individual (the type cannot be changed later). Needs your identity verification. Sources: [Windows Developer Blog](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2025/09/10/free-developer-registration-for-individual-developers-on-microsoft-store/), [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-store-now-lets-individual-developers-join-for-free/). One source disagrees on the company fee, so confirm on Microsoft's page before choosing Company.
3. **Payments in Egypt:** Stripe does not support Egypt as a business country, so do not plan on it. Use **Paymob** (widest methods: cards, wallets, Meeza; about 2.75% + 3 EGP per sale, weekly settlement on the standard plan) and **Fawry** (cash points, reaches customers without cards; fee quoted per merchant). **Kashier** is the alternative with the most store plugins. Typical documents to have ready: national ID (both sides), tax card, Egyptian bank account (IBAN, name matching the ID), sometimes a utility bill; ask the provider's sales team which of these an individual needs, since the requirements are not published in one place. Sources: [payment gateways in Egypt 2026](https://www.kerolosxgad.com/writing/payment-gateways-egypt-comparison), [Stripe in Egypt](https://startglobal.co/us/international/egypt/stripe/), [documents for payment processing](https://xpay.app/blog/documents-needed-payment-processing-egypt). Until the keys exist, every product shows "Subscriptions Launching Soon" and collects emails.
4. **Later, when income pays for it:** domain (Cloudflare Registrar, sold at cost; `.com` is cheaper than `.eg`), Google Play ($25 one-time, verify current price), Apple Developer ($99 a year, verify). None is needed to launch: web, PWA, Windows installer and APK from GitHub Releases come first.
5. **Publisher name "SIR":** use it as the product brand and app names now. An individual account shows your legal name as seller; a company name needs a registered entity. Decide when you register a business; nothing blocks until then.

## What agents prepare so you only click
- Store listings in four languages, privacy pages that match the data really collected, screenshots, support page, age and data-safety answers, MSIX package and winget manifest, and a one-page `docs/STORE_PACKET.md` per product.
- A pre-filled answers sheet (business description, categories, URLs) so every form is copy-paste. The agent opens the signup page in your Chrome and fills everything it can; it stops only at the sign-in, ID check or payment step and tells you exactly which.

# Product direction from the owner (2026-11-12, in chat). Every plan, task, folder, list and page must follow this file.

## 1. SIR Platform is cancelled as a project
It was an idea ("turn websites into apps"). That is simply what we do for EVERY product now: each product ships on every platform (web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, others) from one codebase and one set of shared standards. The shared standards live in plans/SHARED_PLATFORM_KIT.md and standard/OWNER_DEFAULTS.md as internal rules, not as a product. Remove the project folder (move to `D:\Projects\old projects\`, never delete), its Desktop folder, its TickTick task, and every mention as a product (maps, hubs, dashboards, Portfolio site, plans, queue).

## 2. SIR Obour Hub becomes SIR Education Hub
A single education platform for the world, not one institute: anyone can create their own "Hub" (a university, an institute, a school, a training center, a teacher's group, an educational organization) with its own members, roles, courses, schedules, materials, quizzes and practice, chats, announcements, billing and settings; people join the Hubs they belong to and get all features. Obour Institute is the first Hub (the owner's own, with the current Obour features). Absorbs the old ideas: study help, language learning, teaching platform. Available on web, mobile (Android and iOS), Windows and the other platforms, one account everywhere. Free trial for a Hub, then subscription (fair pricing in EGP and USD, free tier for students). Multi-tenant data model, owner-only analytics page. Rename everywhere: SIR Obour Hub to SIR Education Hub. Merge the old Obour plans with this vision into one bigger plan.

## 3. SIR Business is one site with business types
A business owner signs in, then chooses the kind of business (restaurant, pharmacy, library, market and every other sector worth supporting: cafe, clinic, gym, salon, bakery, car service, school shop, retail, wholesale and so on; research and list them), then sets up and configures everything for that business. Free trial first, then a subscription. One owner can add several businesses; each business has its own team and workers, roles and permissions, and a complete management system suited to its sector (inventory, orders, staff, schedules, finance, customers, reports), all connected. We (the company) hold the data about businesses, employees and usage with strict privacy. The old modes (Restaurant, Market, Library, Pharmacy) become sector templates of this one product.

## 4. SIR Hagoozat (bookings) takes the booking family
Football pitch booking and every other booking idea (position coach, other service bookings such as handymen, and similar). Decide per idea: a module of Hagoozat if it is a booking, a separate project if it is a different product. Same premium standard.

## 5. Ideas that were not planned (price and game-deal finder, cars, accessibility map, handyman, movies)
Decide each one: module of an existing product (for example handyman bookings inside Hagoozat) or its own project with the same premium plan, tasks, Desktop folder and TickTick parent task as every other product. Dropped for legal and privacy reasons and staying dropped: citizens program, face rating.

Premium Apple-level quality, Arabic and English, all platforms, owner-only analytics page, subscriptions with a free tier, the AI core with the free-model fallback chain, hosting on Firebase and Vercel now, domains and payments later, Desktop folder kit (standard/OWNER_DEFAULTS.md item 19), competitor matrix, milestones M0 to M6 with acceptance tests, first 10 tasks ready for the agents. Old plans and new plans are merged: read both, keep what is still true.
